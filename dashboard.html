NEW ENTRIES · BOTH STRATEGIES

Risk & lot size

Saved settings apply to new entries; management also applies to existing bot-owned demo positions.
All pairs and both strategies, including manual trades. When reached, new bot entries are skipped; open trades are not touched.

Fixed lot by pair · BBMA + Confluence

Exact broker symbol. A filled value overrides global risk sizing for both strategies. Leave blank to use global risk sizing / launcher fixed lot. New entries only; existing positions and pending orders keep their volume. Broker limits are checked before submission.

Trade time windows · per strategy

Your local PC time. When ON, that strategy opens NEW entries only inside the window; open positions and their management are not affected. Leave OFF for 24 hours. A window may cross midnight (e.g. 22:00 → 03:00; start = end means all day).

Each feature is independent. Partial close must leave a valid remaining lot; 0.01 lot often cannot be split. Break-even is price-based before costs. Stops only tighten. No auto-close from strategy review flags.
